    A stealth game with guards that learn

    Mission Evade

    Slip past the guards and reach the exit while they are all on patrol. Clear 5 security levels before you are caught 3 times. After every run the guards study your route, and each new level adds a guard where you were caught or seen.

    How to play
    • WASD or arrow keys to move. On a touch screen, use the pad.
    • Guards see in a cone. While you stay in it their suspicion fills; when it is full they chase and radio the other guard.
    • Break line of sight and keep moving: a guard gives up after a few seconds and searches where it last saw you.
    • Space throws a decoy (3 per run). It rings for 6 seconds, and guards that hear it go there and search until it stops.
    • The exit only opens while every guard is on patrol. If one is suspicious, investigating or chasing, it is locked (red) until they calm down.
    • Escaping clears a security level and brings in one more guard, posted where you were caught or spotted before. Getting caught costs a life. A new mission resets what the guards learned.
    • Esc pause · R restart run · V camera · ` developer view.
    Settings

    Learner: Local learner. To use Claude, OpenAI or your own endpoint, open the developer view → Learning.

    Paused

    Restarting does not cost a life. Leaving to the menu keeps your mission.
