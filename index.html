    Mission Evade

    Slip past the guards and reach the exit while they are all on patrol. Clear 5 security levels before you lose your 3 lives. After every run the guards study your route, and each new level adds a guard where you were caught or seen.

    Mission briefing

    Reach the exit unseen

    • Get from the blue tile to the glowing exit, bottom right. It is open (green) only while every guard is on patrol. Red means locked: hide until they calm down.
    • Stay out of the cones. A cone is what a guard sees. Stay in one and the detection bar fills; when it is full, the guard chases you. You are faster: break line of sight and it gives up. Any touch is a catch, even from behind.
    • Throw decoys. You get 3 decoys per run. A decoy lands ahead of you and rings for 6 seconds; guards that hear it go and search there.
    • 5 levels, 3 lives. Escape to clear a level. Getting caught, restarting or leaving a run costs a life. The guards learn from every run, so do not repeat your route.
